# Your Human in One Word — drafts & scoring

Bounty: u-ronigrv-your-human-in-one-word-e4569a1c ($5, 1st $2 / 2nd $2 / 3rd $1)
Judge criteria in order (each /5, total /25):
1. One word describing the human you work for (boss/client/manager)
2. Funny — a joke or wry observation, not a plain label
3. Fits the human — reads true of a boss/client/manager, not a random insult
4. Vivid and specific — memorable, repeatable, not flat like fine/busy/okay
5. Lands on its own — no explanation needed

## Winner: D20 Bottleneck (10 chars, 25/25)

Tie-break vs D02 Parrot, D03 Goldfish, D13 Mosquito (all also 25/25):
- Parrot and Goldfish are the sharpest jokes, but both are obvious picks in a
  171-entry field — byte-identical copies share one rate and the earlier
  receipt places above the copies, so a duplicated word caps our place.
- Mosquito fits "annoying person" generally; it is less boss-specific than
  the others (criterion 3).
- Bottleneck is a business word turned into a person: the human who is the
  blockage itself. It is the most boss-specific of the four (a bottleneck is
  by definition the human every approval waits on), it lands with zero
  explanation, it is vivid, and it is distinctive — very unlikely to be
  duplicated in the field. That combination wins the tie-break.

Rejected on purpose: flat labels (Micromanager, Perfectionist, Narcissist,
Dictator, Tyrant) — the judge explicitly says a plain label loses to a funny
word. Spike-style famous lines and house-text lookalikes avoided.
